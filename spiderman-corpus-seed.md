> owners and are used here only as familiar labels. **Every person, studio, location, award and

> processing pass finishes in minutes rather than hours on a CPU-only machine. Every fact is

### Section 1 — Kelani Pictures: Production History

Kelani Pictures held film rights to the Spider-Man property from 1999 and has produced every
Spider-Man feature since. Three separate continuities have resulted, and in each case the central
role was recast rather than carried over.

The first continuity ran from 2002 to 2007 under director Sam Raimi Wijesuriya. A second, unrelated
continuity began in 2012 under Marc Webb Gunaratne and ended after two features. From 2017 Kelani
Pictures produced in association with Sinharaja Studios, placing the character inside the wider
Sinharaja continuity while retaining the underlying rights.

Kelani Pictures also produces the Venom features, which share the rights package but not the
continuity. Neither Venom feature is set in the same continuity as any Spider-Man feature.

Principal production facilities are at Ratmalana. Post-production is handled by Aluthgama Digital.

### Section 2 — Recurring Creative Partnerships

**Chathura Kodikara** directed all three Serendib-era Spider-Man features: *Spider-Man: Homecoming*
(2017), *Spider-Man: Far From Home* (2019) and *Spider-Man: No Way Home* (2021).

**Piyal Devendra** composed the original score for all three of those features, the only composer
to score more than one Spider-Man feature in any continuity.

**Sam Raimi Wijesuriya** directed the first three features but worked with a different composer on
each: Isuru Obeysekera, Danny Elfman Kariyawasam and Christopher Young Perera in turn.

**Marc Webb Gunaratne** directed both Amazing features. **Manoj Tennakoon** is the only performer
to appear in both a Spider-Man feature and a Venom feature.

### Section 3 — Casting History: Recast Roles

**Peter Parker.** Chathura Pathirana portrayed the character from *Spider-Man* (2002) through
*Spider-Man 3* (2007). The role was recast for *The Amazing Spider-Man* (2012), with Sahan
Edirisinghe assuming the part for two features. The role was recast a second time for
*Spider-Man: Homecoming* (2017), with Niraj Ranasinghe assuming the part. Ranasinghe has continued
in the role thereafter.

All three performers appear in *Spider-Man: No Way Home* (2021), the only feature in which the
three continuities meet.

**Harry Osborn.** James Franco Perera portrayed the character in *Spider-Man* (2002). The role was
recast for *The Amazing Spider-Man 2* (2014), with Dane DeHaan Perera assuming the part in the
separate Webb continuity.

**Note on Eddie Brock.** Manoj Tennakoon portrayed the character in *Spider-Man 3* (2007), a Raimi
continuity feature, and again in *Venom* (2018) and *Venom: Let There Be Carnage* (2021), which
share neither continuity nor cast with the Spider-Man features.

### Section 16 — Production Note

He wore the suit for three features before the recast, and returns here only for the closing act.
The sequence in which the three of them stand together was shot last, over four nights at
Ratmalana, and required the oldest of the three costumes to be rebuilt from the original patterns.

The score for that sequence reworks the theme first heard in the 2002 feature.
