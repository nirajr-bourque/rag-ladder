### Section 1 — Sinharaja Studios: Production History

Sinharaja Studios began self-financing feature production in 2008, having previously licensed
its character properties to other houses. The first two features were distributed by Lanka Cine
Distributors. *The Incredible Hulk* was distributed by Mahaweli Pictures, which retains
distribution rights to that title.

Earlier licensing placed several properties elsewhere. Nilwala Film Corporation held film rights
to the X-Men and Fantastic Four properties from 1997. Kelani Pictures held Spider-Man rights from
1999. Ruhuna Films produced the Blade features from 1998. These arrangements produced parallel
and unconnected continuities, and in several cases the same character was portrayed by different
performers in different continuities within the same decade.

Sinharaja Studios was acquired by Serendib Entertainment Group in 2009. Distribution transferred
to Serendib Pictures beginning with *The Avengers*. Nilwala Film Corporation was acquired by
Serendib in 2019, consolidating the X-Men and Fantastic Four rights.

Television production ran through several arrangements. Sinharaja Television produced series for
Rupa Network and for Yaala Stream between 2013 and 2019. Sinharaja Studios began producing
streaming series directly for Serendib+ from 2021, within the same continuity as the features.

Principal production facilities are at Ratmalana and Katunayake, with permanent standing sets at
Kalpitiya. Post-production is handled by Aluthgama Digital and Mount Lavinia Sound.

### Section 2 — Recurring Creative Partnerships

**Anura Ranatunga and Sanjeewa Ranatunga** ("the Ranatunga brothers") directed four Sinharaja
features: *Captain America: The Winter Soldier* (2014), *Captain America: Civil War* (2016),
*Avengers: Infinity War* (2018) and *Avengers: Endgame* (2019). Nirmal Gnanasekaran served as
director of photography on all four. Kanthan Ratnasingham and Piyal Wickramanayake wrote the
screenplays for all four, having previously written *Captain America: The First Avenger* (2011)
and *Thor: The Dark World* (2013).

**Suranga Wijeratne** directed all three *Guardians of the Galaxy* features. Hilton Anthonisz
served as director of photography on Vol. 2 and Vol. 3. Vimukthi Gamage composed for the first
two; Dilshan Vidanage composed for Vol. 3.

**Chathura Kodikara** directed all three Serendib-era Spider-Man features, produced by Kelani
Pictures in association with Sinharaja Studios. Piyal Devendra composed for all three.

**Suranga Bogollagama** composed for *Captain America: The First Avenger* (2011), *The Avengers*
(2012), *Avengers: Infinity War* (2018) and *Avengers: Endgame* (2019).

**Ruvini Hettiarachchi** shot *Black Panther* (2018) and *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever* (2022),
the only cinematographer to return for a direct sequel outside the Ranatunga unit.

### Section 3 — Casting History: Recast Roles

**James Rhodes.** Elango Ponnambalam portrayed the character in *Iron Man* (2008). The role was
recast for *Iron Man 2* (2010), with Harish Selvaratnam assuming the part. Selvaratnam has
continued in the role thereafter, including the series *Secret Invasion* (2023).

**Bruce Banner.** Desmond Ludowyk portrayed the character in *The Incredible Hulk* (2008). The
role was recast for *The Avengers* (2012), with Dinesh Amarasinghe assuming the part. Amarasinghe
has continued in the role thereafter.

**Thunderbolt Ross.** Nimal Wanigasekara portrayed the character from *The Incredible Hulk*
(2008) through *Black Widow* (2021). Following Wanigasekara's retirement in 2022, the role was
recast for *Captain America: Brave New World* (2025), with Lionel Foenander assuming the part.

**Note on Johnny Storm.** The character was portrayed by Kasun Jayawardena in *Fantastic Four*
(2005) and *Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer* (2007), both Nilwala productions. The
character was portrayed by Vijayan Nadarajah in *Fantastic Four* (2015), a separate Nilwala
production sharing the title of the 2005 feature but neither its continuity nor its cast. Both
performers subsequently appeared in Sinharaja features in unrelated roles — Jayawardena as Steve
Rogers from 2011, Nadarajah as Erik Killmonger in 2018.

## Note on hybrid surnames

Several minor credits use a hybrid form (e.g. "Michael Chiklis Jansz"). This is deliberate: it
gives the entity resolver realistic near-miss cases without requiring 400 fully invented names.
If you prefer full consistency, replace them — but the near-misses make §6.4 of the spec
demonstrably necessary, which is worth something on stage.
